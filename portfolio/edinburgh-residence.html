The Project

Warmth at Every Scale

This New Jersey estate called for a full-home design concept that could feel simultaneously grounded and elevated — warm enough for family life, refined enough to make an impression. Each room was designed as a distinct environment while maintaining a unified material language across the entire home.

Natural stone, dark-stained wood, layered textiles, and a palette drawn from earth and shadow form the backbone of the design. Furniture selections balance sculptural presence with livability, and lighting was curated to anchor each space with character.
