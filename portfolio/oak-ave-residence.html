The Project

A Room Worth Coming Back To

This historic Colonial presented a compelling design challenge: the original architecture — dark wood wainscoting, stained glass transoms, and high ceilings — demanded to be respected, not erased. The goal was a living room that felt genuinely transformed while still belonging to the house.

Two paint directions were explored: Sherwin-Williams Frosted Fern, a soft sage that reads as fresh and botanical, and Storm Cloud, a sophisticated mid-tone grey with blue undertones. Each direction was paired with coordinating Roman shade and wallpaper selections to create a cohesive window treatment story. Furniture selections lean traditional in silhouette — tufted wingbacks, velvet sofas, skirted chairs — but are curated with a restraint that keeps the room from tipping into period pastiche.
