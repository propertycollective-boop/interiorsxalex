New Jersey Residence

Full-Home Renovation  ·  Design Concept

Location New Jersey
Project Type Full-Home Renovation
Scope Entire Home
Style Transitional · Warm Neutral

Project Overview

This full-home renovation concept was designed around one clear intention: transform a dated interior into a cohesive, elevated residence that feels both timeless and deeply livable. Every room was approached holistically — from the foyer's curved staircase to the master bath's Calacatta marble — with a palette rooted in whites, warm neutrals, and natural textures throughout.

The client's vision centered on open-concept flow, custom millwork and molding, and a complete kitchen redesign with a wine wall and butler's pantry. The result is a home that balances architectural detail with material warmth.

Scope of Work

  • Foyer & Staircase redesign
  • Full kitchen renovation
  • Wine wall & butler's pantry
  • Dining room concept
  • Living room with custom built-ins
  • Breakfast / dining nook
  • Playroom design
  • Master bedroom suite
  • Master bath renovation
  • Guest bath renovation
  • Wainscoting & custom molding throughout
  • Hardwood flooring whole-home

Materials & Palette

The Foundation

White oak flooring, Calacatta Gold marble, fluted white molding, gray stone, and warm brass finishes establish a cohesive material language across every room.

Foyer & Staircase

The Grand Entrance

A curved staircase railing and arched doorway set the architectural tone at entry. Custom wainscoting and panel molding carry through into the hallways, anchoring the home's transitional identity.

Kitchen

The Heart of the Home

A complete kitchen renovation with warm wood cabinetry, a marble backsplash, brass pot filler, and a dedicated wine wall with Crittall-style glass cabinet. The breakfast nook anchors casual dining with a round oak table and banquette seating.

Dining Room

Elevated Gathering

A range of dining table options explored — from sleek black to travertine stone — all anchored by sculptural seating and statement lighting overhead.

Living Room

Open & Layered

Coffered ceilings, a cream sectional, linen drapes, and flanking built-ins around the fireplace define a living room that is both airy and architectural.

Lighting

Finishing Details

Brass chandeliers, pendant drops, and gallery-wall picture lights were curated to add warmth and texture throughout — never an afterthought.

Playroom

Made for Play

A dedicated children's space designed with warm neutrals, custom built-in storage, and a play kitchen — beautiful enough to share the aesthetic of the whole home.

Master Suite

A Quiet Retreat

The master bedroom wraps around an all-white and cream palette — layered bouclé, sculptural pendants, sheer drapes, and an upholstered headboard. The ensuite bath follows with Calacatta marble, a freestanding soaking tub, and gold wall-mount fixtures.

Guest Bath

Spa-Inspired

The guest bathroom carries the same material sophistication — fluted vanity fronts, stone vessel sinks, gold wall-mount faucets, and Calacatta marble shower surrounds.

"Every material choice, every molding detail — it all adds up to a home that feels curated without being cold."
— Alexandra Hurwitz, Interiors x Alex
