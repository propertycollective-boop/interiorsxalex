The Brief

Open, warm,
entirely theirs

The clients came to Alex with a clear feeling in mind — open, airy, and deeply personal. The existing home had great bones but a fragmented layout that worked against how the family actually lived.

Alex's design concept centered on opening the main floor into a true open-concept living space, introducing custom millwork, arched doorways, and a warm neutral palette throughout. Natural textures — travertine, linen, aged oak — ground every room while keeping the overall feel light.

Palette: Off White · Linen · Sand · Warm Taupe · Espresso · Brushed Brass
