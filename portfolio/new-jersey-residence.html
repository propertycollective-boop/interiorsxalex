The Project

Refined from the Ground Up

This full-home renovation was designed around a clear vision: open-concept flow, whites and neutrals, natural textures, and architectural detail that carries throughout every room. Custom molding and wainscoting tie the foyer, dining room, hallways, and living spaces together, while new hardwood flooring runs seamlessly from entry to master suite.

The kitchen received a complete redesign — personalized cabinetry, a butler's pantry, and considered hardware details. Both bathrooms were fully remodeled with Calacatta marble and brass fixtures. Built-ins anchor the living room and playrooms, and a custom walk-in closet was added to the primary suite.
